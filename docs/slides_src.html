Throughline

Your weekly step-back: five minutes to see the bigger picture of your life, in your own words.

For people in a demanding season. Save small moments as you go. When a week blurs together, press Zoom Out to see what it held, what's quietly slipping, and how you've grown. You shape the story.

The problem we're solving

People in a demanding season of life capture more of it than ever, yet rarely step back to see what it adds up to. The weeks blur together, and they can't easily tell whether the way they spend their days still matches what they care about, until they've already drifted.

Today's tools don't close the gap: photo apps resurface by date, journals go unread, and goal apps add pressure.

How might we give busy people five minutes a week to see the bigger picture of their lives, in their own words?

Safety · built and tested

Four risks, each with a defense that's been tested

  • Injection: the scanner holds it back, entries reach the model only as escaped data, and tools are read-only. In a live attack with the scanner off, the provider blocked the blatant one and the model ignored the subtle one.
  • Invented memories: code verifies every quote against its entry.
  • Crisis: resources (988), never AI analysis, and Zoom Out pauses.
  • Spend: caps per run, per day and per agent.
